01

ROOK

STRAIGHT LINES

The rook

Moves straight up, down or sideways, any distance.

REMEMBER · It stops when it captures. The dark piece ends its line.

02

BISHOP

DIAGONALS ONLY

The bishop

Moves diagonally, any distance. Always stays on the same square color.

REMEMBER · This bishop started on a dark square, so every square it lights up is dark.

03

QUEEN

ANY DIRECTION

The queen

Moves straight or diagonally, any distance.

REMEMBER · Rook lines and bishop lines, all from one square.

04

KNIGHT

THE L-SHAPE

The knight

Moves in an L shape: two squares one way and one to the side. It can jump over pieces.

REMEMBER · Count 2, then 1. It lands on the opposite color each time.

05

KING

ONE SQUARE

The king

Moves one square in any direction, but never into danger.

REMEMBER · Never step onto a square an enemy piece can reach.

06

PAWN

FORWARD ONLY

The pawn

Moves forward one square, or two from its starting square if the way is clear. It captures one square diagonally forward. Never backward.

REMEMBER · Straight to move, diagonal to capture.

07

ONE RULE

FOR EVERY PIECE

One shared rule

Only the knight can jump over pieces. You can't land on your own piece, and capturing an opponent's piece ends your move there.

SPECIAL MOVES · Castling, en passant and promotion can wait until these feel familiar.
